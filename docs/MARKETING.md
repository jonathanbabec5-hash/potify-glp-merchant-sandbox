# Green Light Pay Merchant Sandbox marketing and sales plan

**Owner.** Chance coordinates public materials. The GLP project owner must confirm any commercial agreement.

**Audience.** Point-of-sale engineers and pilot merchants. **Offer.** Let an operator inspect how a checkout request moves from counter to receipt.

| Proposed timing | Public material | Desired outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–3 | Publish an annotated sequence for one fictional counter sale and its reconciliation fields. | Give the audience one verifiable reason to try the project. |
| Days 4–10 | Share the reviewed demo or sample through the project page and one relevant creator or community channel. | Review the receipt contract. |
| Days 11–28 | Publish a build note showing what the trial revealed and one change made from feedback. | 3 merchant workflow corrections. |

### Sales test

Ask three merchants to annotate a fictional receipt. A future fixed-fee reconciliation pilot must name its merchant, deliverable, provider and acceptance criteria. Offer a scoped GLP discovery session only after a merchant confirms a concrete reconciliation problem.

### Measurement and decision

Use `glp-merchant-sandbox-first-public-build` for the pilot. Record a 28-day prelaunch baseline where available, then observe each participant for seven days after their first visit. Current audience, conversion and net revenue baselines are unknown. The numbers above are proposed targets. Distinguish visits, qualified actions, buyer inquiries and paid receipts. Keep private identifiers in the authorized reporting system. If the outcome cannot be attributed, label it unknown.

Proposed pilot budget is $0 for distribution and two founder hours for the first demonstration. At day 7, inspect the biggest point of friction. At day 28, expand only if the target action was measured and the sales test produced identifiable demand. Report fulfilled sales net of refunds and direct costs. A product serving trust or internal operations may have no direct sales at all.

**Prerequisite for external promotion.** Use the restored server and a fictional checkout for the developer demo. Describe provider partnerships or live settlement only when separately verified. Draft copy and placements go through the existing approval process.
